How to play

Gold. Your capital, towns, workshops and ports pay gold every turn. Nothing is automatic: click a hex and spend it in the Selected hex card, or on Research.

Troops cost 1 gold each, raised in any calm town or port you own. Troops raised this turn cannot move until next turn; if they join an army, only the troops that were already there can move.

Splitting. With an army selected, pick ¼, ½, ¾ or All under the map before you move. Each side of a split must have at least 10 troops. The part left behind can still move later this turn. With Auto-garrison on, an army leaving a town in unrest leaves about a quarter behind (at least 10).

Moves. 5 per turn, +1 per 3 towns (max 12). Land armies go 2 hexes, further along roads. Each army moves once per turn.

Battles. Power is size × morale factor. The loser is wiped out, but a failed attack still bleeds the defender.

Morale is the small number, 1 to 5: 1 fights at 80%, 3 at 100%, 5 at 120%. It belongs to each army: +1 for beating an army of 8+, repelling one, or taking a town; everyone gets +1 when you take a capital. Merging averages it. Low morale recovers to 3 in a calm town.

Works. Farms (your land within 2 of your town), mills (rivers), mines (mountains with gold dots) and fisheries (sea) pay gold, and pay more when linked to a mill, a workshop town or a port: within 2 hexes, or any distance along your own road. They are outlined in their owner's color and can be burned for half their cost: a farm when its hex changes hands, the others when an enemy army ends its move next to them.

Walls add defense and post a militia, so an empty walled town must be stormed. Capitals start with level 2 walls.

Unrest. A town taken from someone pays nothing for 3 turns and may revolt if no army holds it.

Supply. An army whose land is not connected to one of your towns (red dashed ring) loses 1 morale a turn, then troops.

Capitals. Taking one eliminates that nation and loots half its gold. Its garrisoned towns turn rebel; the rest of its land goes free.

Sea. Armies embark at ports or wharves, sail 3, and land on any coast (defender +3). The map's ports are full towns: gold, recruiting, walls, workshops, unrest. A wharf you build is only a launch point.

Players. Set each color to Human or AI in the header. With two or more humans the game asks you to pass the device at each turn.
